Voice rules+
  • Use first person. Tamien’s story is an asset, not a side note.
  • Build then land. Set up the thought, then deliver a short line that hits.
  • Use frameworks lightly. They should simplify, not overwhelm.
  • Name the hard truth. Warm does not mean soft.
  • Always include application. The reader should know what to do next.
Signature language to preserve+
A better you is better for everybody.

What to avoid+

Avoid generic motivation, AI-polished corporate language, over-explaining frameworks, and content that sounds like Think 3D wrote it instead of Tamien. The best content should feel like a smart friend who has lived the lesson and can explain the system behind it.
